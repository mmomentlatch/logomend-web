FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good to know

Is my video uploaded to a server?

No. The file is processed in your browser memory with WebAssembly. We never receive its contents.

Which formats and resolutions are supported?

MP4, MOV and WebM work best. 720p, 1080p, 2K and 4K are supported, but large videos require more memory.

Why is Web slower than Local?

Browsers isolate processing and impose stricter memory limits. Local uses native FFmpeg and is better for large files.

Will the logo disappear perfectly?

The result depends on the background, motion and logo size. OpenCV and FFmpeg rebuild the selected area from neighboring pixels; very complex moving backgrounds are better handled by Local AI modes.

Can I remove any watermark?

Only process your own material or video you have permission to edit. Removing someone else’s attribution may violate their rights.

Can I add an intro or outro and trim the video precisely?

Yes. Add a JPG, PNG or WebP image at the beginning or end, choose its duration, and enter exact trim times in minutes and seconds.
